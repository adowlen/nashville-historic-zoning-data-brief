## Held copy - lead with the killer number

90.1% of Nashville historic-zoning cases accepted since 2021 are recorded as approved. Cases accepted 2011-2020 read 1.4%.

9,171 cases, 2011-2026. 59.8% of dated cases issued the same day; cases that took at least a day waited a median of 17 days. New Construction is the slow subtype: median 13 days. One ZIP - 37206 - holds 37.9% of all cases. And 4,602 cases recorded as "Disapproved" carry an issued date, which is why the approval share is only reported on the post-2020 base.

Full brief, data, and audit: https://github.com/adowlen/nashville-historic-zoning-data-brief
